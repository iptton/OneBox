# Code of Conduct

This is a small project maintained by one person. The rules are short. They are adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language. Assume good intent, especially across languages and time zones.
- Give and accept technical criticism gracefully. "This will deadlock under X" is helpful; "this is stupid" is not.
- Respect that maintainer time is finite. A clear, minimal report gets a faster answer than a long one.
- Accept that a rejected PR is not a personal judgement. Scope, not quality, is the most common reason something is not merged here.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.
- Dismissing a report because of the reporter's nationality, language, or English fluency.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when you are representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/wangzhishou/OneBox/security/advisories/new) (it is private even for non-security reports) or contact [@wangzhishou](https://github.com/wangzhishou) directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, the maintainer may respond with a private warning, a request to edit or delete a comment, a temporary block from the repository, or a permanent ban. There is no committee and no appeals process — this is a one-person project, and that is the honest description of how it works.
